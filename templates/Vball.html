What is NPP and what is it used for

NPP is the stepping stone for playing volleyball on the international level, after making your teams state you play at nationals, when you play at nationals coaches are looking at your abilities NPP is invitation only program, it is a 7 day camp where the best players are picked for Australian devolpement team program. The best of these players will be picked to go represent australia at the AVC.
